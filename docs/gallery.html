Moving the threshold trades misses for false alarms

Dot plotDraggable thresholdSettings

A detector scores every case from 0 to 1 and flags anything at or above a threshold. At a threshold of , it catches real problems and raises false alarms. Drag the line, or the number. How much the two groups overlap is in the figure's settings.

A focus pulls in only what is near it

Particles follow a smooth flow. Inside the ring they are pulled toward its center; outside it, nothing changes. Drag the ring to move it and its knob to resize it. Strength and field scale are in the settings.
